## Open questions and pitfalls
- Do not cite Conjecture C (block 7) or the unrestricted Conjecture C″ (block 9 first form). The $|T| = 1$, $|U| = 1$ strata are safe for a different reason (vanishing cell variance), not by the count.
- `cm_check_strata`'s `worst` / `slack` are only informative when a stratum violates (the count stops at `need`); remove the early break if you want the true minimum slack (costs $\approx n$ times more at \times5$).
- The least-squares finder of step 26 slides to the stratum boundary (some $ or \to0$) when the system is unsolvable; always check `full`.
- `cm_obj` (Schur form) is undefined along the cross paths ($\Omega_{MM}$ singular); use the direct observed-cell form (`Fobs` in step 25/26).
- Appending rows to an experiment grid preserves seeds; inserting changes them. Foreground `sleep` is blocked; a Python `time.sleep` loop works for waiting on background jobs.
