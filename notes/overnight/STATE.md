## Open questions and pitfalls
- The count rule's ε̄ is estimated from kept matrices; with casewise contamination at exactly 1 − α_obs the binomial correction over-corrects by one count unit (test 21 documents it).
- Structural casewise outliers + cellwise contamination: not identifiable in the cellwise paradigm (JOURNAL block 5); say so in the paper and point to the hard quota.
- The location's forced marginal flags (Wfix) are computed once from the median-centred data; under > 20 % shifted matrices the median centre is off, but the EM location and the trimming recover (cm_step14 shiftmat error 0.32).
- Inserting a row in the middle of an experiment grid changes the seeds of the later rows (seeds are 4000 + 100·g + r): append new rows at the end.
- Each block's start-up reading costs ≈ $2–3 of the cap; keep reads to this file, the brief, METHOD §4e/§7–9, the block-5 JOURNAL entries.
- `pgrep -f` inside a waiting loop matches the loop's own shell; wait on a done marker. Foreground `sleep` is blocked for the agent: wait with a background `until` loop.
