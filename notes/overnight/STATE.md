## Open questions and pitfalls
- Prop. B's bound uses the min eigenvector of Σ^r as the test vector; it fails to see degenerations whose vanishing directions are b_⊥ ⊗ u (scale migration), which are supported only on matrices with ≥ 2 fully kept columns. Do not claim coercivity of the joint problem.
- The block-2 "degenerate" classification (F < F₀ − 50 n) is an overfitting signature at tiny n; do not cite `cm_step5_exist.log` as evidence of unboundedness. The §4a theorem stands (adversarial patterns).
- The weak case (struct + cells) is a small-pq phenomenon; at 8×8 the soft default handles it. Say so in the paper rather than treating it as a general limit.
- Inserting a row in the middle of an experiment grid changes the seeds of later rows: append new rows at the end.
