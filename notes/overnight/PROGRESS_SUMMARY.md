**Block 0: planning (2026-11-04).** Infrastructure set up: setup script, cost meter, stop guard, protocol, and brief. The literature dossier is `notes/00_setting_matrix_cellwise.md`. No research results yet.

**Block 1 (2026-11-04, 12:54–13:16 UTC, ≈ $9).** Method defined and implemented: **cellMMCD**, the cellMCD objective (trimmed observed-cell likelihood, no imputation) with the Kronecker constraint. The Schur identity det Σ_OO = det Σ · det Ω_MM makes it O(p²q + pq² + m³) per observation instead of O((pq)³). Algorithm: exact cell step + score fixed-point parameter step; monotone and convergent (proven, tested; boundedness under the quota still conjectured). Pairwise Kronecker start `kpair`. 8 new tests pass, core 31/31. Benchmark (n = 100, 6-sd cells): recall 1.00, false flags 1–2 %; KL at 5×5, 10 % bad cells: cellMMCD 0.32 vs cellMCD-on-vec 0.59, RJ 89, MMCD 119, MLE 144; at 20 %: 0.44 vs 1.14 and > 280. Clean-data cost: KL 0.37 vs MLE 0.16, mostly a 10 % scale shrink (tr ratio 0.895) from the 1 % tail trimming; the naive 1-D factor (0.925) does not explain it. Run time 0.1–0.7 s at n = 100, 10 s at 10×10 (pure R). Dead ends: product-set deletion and a trimmed pseudo-likelihood (rejected on paper, journal). Next: consistency factor inside the iteration, existence condition, casewise + cellwise mix.

**Block 2 (2026-11-04, 13:13–13:50 UTC, ≈ $10).** (1) The clean-data scale loss is explained: the cell step and the parameter step form a shrink-and-flag loop whose fixed point is scale 0.905 with 1.4 % flagged (1-D proxy, proven; observed 0.895 / 1.7 %). Fix: λ-calibration (λ from c/t₁ + log t₁, final Σ/t₁ with t₁ = 0.925), which keeps the objective and its convergence proof; tr ratio now 0.98–0.99, clean KL at n = 1000 down 3×. (2) Casewise outliers: cells alone fail for 20 % shifted matrices (KL 2.5, the shift hides in the conditional means). Added an observation step to the same objective (threshold on the kept cells with their own χ² dof, quota, and "more than half flagged ⇒ trim"): 20 % shifted / scaled / structural matrices give KL 0.37 / 0.28 / 0.30 vs MMCD 0.21 / 0.27 / 0.23; mixed 10 % matrices + 5 % cells 0.25 vs MMCD 51. Clean cost 0.31 vs MMCD 0.18. Hard-quota variant implemented (needs its consistency factor). (3) Existence: proven that the objective is unbounded below whenever n − h ≥ n/max(p,q) (any cellwise trimmed likelihood with a per-position quota; cellMCD included); the estimator is the local minimiser from the robust start; degenerate descents only at n ≤ 12 in the grid. (4) Efficiency gap at the Gaussian: KL 2.4× the MLE at quant .99, 1.3× at .999; not caused by the start or λ; cause open. Tests 13/13 + 31/31. Dead ends: a fixed observation threshold with pq dof (over-trims under cellwise contamination); the observed-cell threshold alone (misses mostly-flagged shifted matrices).

**Block 3 (2026-11-04, 13:40–13:58 UTC, ≈ $8).** (1) The "efficiency gap" of block 2 is a **bias**: the raw cellMMCD at quant .99 is inconsistent in shape at the Gaussian. Reason (proven to first order): a flagged clean cell is a tail cell of its conditional, but the parameter step treats it as missing, so the fixed point is Σ − κ·diag(1/Ω_cc) with κ = F₁(c) − F₃(c) = 0.0745, which is not a uniform scale. Numerically: KL → 0.0115 as n → ∞ (3×4), predicted 0.0111 by the iterated population map; n·KL grows 18 → 32 → 93 for n = 500 → 8000, against ≈ 8 for the MLE. (2) Remedy adopted as the **new default: reweighting** (flags from the raw .99 fit, re-decided at .999, refit with flags fixed, scale factor t_rw), the analogue of the reweighted MCD: KL ratio to the MLE 1.0–1.5 up to n = 8000, recall 0.96 at 3-sd cells (quant .999 alone: 0.94). A censored parameter step (tail second moment, Fisher-consistent in the 1-D proxy) removed only two thirds of the bias and is not monotone; kept as an option. (3) Benchmarks rerun with the final default: clean KL 0.095 / 0.205 (3×4 / 5×5; MMCD 0.087 / 0.183, MLE 0.070 / 0.155), 10 % bad cells 0.096 / 0.209 (cellMCD-on-vec 0.26 / 0.59, MMCD 50 / 119); casewise 20 % shifted matrices 0.21 (MMCD 0.21), mixed 0.23 (MMCD 51). (4) Dead end: the full-column safeguard against degenerate descents does not prevent them at n ≤ 12 and breaks monotonicity (kept as an option, off). Pitfall: with 10 % cells at 3–4 sd in every matrix the observation step trims its full 25 % of matrices. Tests 17/17 + 31/31. Plot: `output/cm_step6_bias.png`.

**Block 4 (2026-11-04, 13:57–14:45 UTC, ≈ $9).** (1) The block-3 pitfall (25 % of matrices trimmed under 10 % cells at 3–4 sd) was a lock-in: trimmed matrices never had their cells re-decided. Fix: a **shadow cell step** for trimmed matrices during the first 10 iterations (trimmed 0 %, clean 5×5 KL 0.198 → 0.182, clean matrices trimmed 4 % → 0 %). It exposed a second problem: scaled / structural casewise outliers were re-admitted after their worst cells were flagged (selection; KL up to 0.41). Fix: an **adaptive count rule** (at most qbinom(.999, pq, ε̄) flagged cells per kept matrix): scale ×3 matrices trimmed 100 % again (KL 0.19 / 0.22 at 10 / 20 %), structural 0.18 / 0.32 (MMCD 0.18 / 0.23: the remaining weak case), mixed 0.22 (MMCD 51). Cellwise benchmark rerun: 5×5 clean 0.198, 5 / 10 / 20 % cells 0.18 / 0.21 / 0.32 (cellMCD-on-vec 0.63 / 0.59 / 1.14). (2) **Influence functions** of the raw and reweighted estimator derived in the 1-D proxy and checked to 1e-4 against exact contaminated functionals: bounded, gross-error sensitivity 7.4 / 10.0, ≈ 0 beyond the cutoff (MLE: unbounded). (3) **C++ cell step** (RcppArmadillo, identical results): 20×20, n = 100 in 8.6 s instead of 38.8 s (MMCD 4.7 s); 10×10 in 0.4 s. (4) Operating range n ≥ 3·max(p, q): degenerate descents only at n = 8–10 for 4×4. Tests 20/20 + 31/31. Dead ends: no observation trimming (KL 1.96 at 20 % shifted matrices), quant_obs .999 (under-trims), shadow step without the count rule.
